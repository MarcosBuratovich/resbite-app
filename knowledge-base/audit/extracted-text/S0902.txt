Features 
Product 
Location suggestions 
We help you to find locations for activi ties for the times when you know what  to do but don’t know where to go. 
Resbite chat 
Choose to chat with either your resbite’s  invitee list or just those that have con firmed attendance. 
Covid compliance 
An option to make sure your resbite is  conforming to the latest government  rules and guidance for your locality. 
Flexible invitations 
Instead of a time-specific resbite, there’s  a connection-first resbite. It creates a  poll to find a time all invitees can attend. 
Photo collections 
Collate photos taken by all attendees  during your resbite into a central reposi tory for download. 
Weather checker 
If you’re planning an outdoor activity,  you’ll be able to check the weather fore cast for an activity’s time and location. 
Groups & favorites 
Make things easier by grouping individu al connections as well as adding activities  and locations to favourites. 
Guru stories 
Hear the real-life stories of experts for each  activity, why they love what they do and  how it helped their health and wellbeing. 
Hosted resbites 
Find national and local premium resbites,  invite your connections and purchase tick ets directly from within the app.
