### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide11.xml
TO access the premium features you'll need a premium subscription.
A subscription costs £6.99 a month or £39.99 a year
Premium subscriptions will account for 100% of our revenue in the first version of the app but then reduce to 25% of the second version.
### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide12.xml
The second version of the app will include hosted resbites. This will make up the vast majority of our future revenues.
Hosted resbites are pre-completed with an activity, time and place from a host where all you need to do is add your friends and then purchase your ticket.
Examples of a hosted resbite could be a national event such as Glastonbury or hyper-local events such as a yoga class in a village hall.
We would look to take a 15% commission of hosted resbite sales.
### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide13.xml
The global COVID lockdowns have given us an opportunity to research the human health and wellbeing effects of the virtual vs the real. We partnered with Staffordshire university for this research.
In the UK, the top 4 wants identified for people who had all their basic needs met were (1) to be outside, (2) to be reconnected to family and friends,(3) to take a holiday and (4) to take up a new hobby or interest. Our app caters for all of these things.
A post COVID launch for us is perfect timing.

### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide3.xml
The overuse and misuse of tech is impacting our health and wellbeing
We’re basically turning to devices to conduct our conversations and relationships and therefore avoiding human interaction and real-life experiences.
To give you some examples of how this affects us: 75% of UK children now spend less time outside than prison inmates and studies on children that used smartphones for more than 5 hours a day show their levels of empathy and face-reading abilities are borderline autistic.
Existing big social tech business models are based on the attention economy
This means they make more money; the more people stay and interact with their platform and so it forces the apps design to be unethical and create addiction. Because the problems with social tech start from the business model, existing platforms will never be able to organically change for the better.
