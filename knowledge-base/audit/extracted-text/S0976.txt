### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide14.xml
The second version of the app will include hosted resbites. This will make up the vast majority of our future revenues.
Hosted resbites are pre-completed with an activity, time and place from a host, where all you need to do is add your friends and then purchase your ticket.
Examples of a hosted resbite could be a national event such as Glastonbury or a hyper-local event such as a yoga class in a village hall.
We would look to take a 15% commission of hosted resbite sales.
### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide15.xml
The global COVID lockdowns have given us an opportunity to research the human health and wellbeing effects of the virtual vs the real. We partnered with Staffordshire university for this research.
In the UK, the top 4 wants identified for people who had all their basic needs met were (1) to be outside, (2) to be reconnected to family and friends, (3) to take a holiday and (4) to take up a new hobby or interest. Our app caters for all of these things.
A post COVID launch for us is perfect timing.

### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide3.xml
So, the problem starts with…
The ever-increasing use of devices to conduct our conversations and therefore the avoidance of human interaction and real-life experiences.
Existing big social tech business models 
need people to stay and interact with their platform to make more money which leads to addictive design.
And any new innovations in social technology normally pulls people further away from reality which 
is counter-intuitive to the very purpose of social. 
Human to human relationships are either being replaced with human to tech relationships or having the tech placed in-between them. 
### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide4.xml
At resbite...
We encourage human-to-human connection and the removal of technology wherever possible.
 
Resbite is a closed private network so there is no purpose for bots or fake profiles, no connections to trolling strangers, no way to compare the way you look with manipulated identities and no confidence harming features such as like buttons.
We’re removing any addictive design which includes 
unnecessary notifications, user tagging of photos or posts, virtual gamification and any non-stop scrolling.
We enable better privacy by not running 
ads, so are not dependent on detailed user data for revenue. 
Overall, we believe that in order to make a sustainable business we need to align our business model with helping people. The happier and healthier our customers are, the more financially successful we’ll be.
