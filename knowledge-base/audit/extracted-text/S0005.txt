Taking inspiration from the icon and the concept behind it;
balancing time, I have created a graphic device that could be
used across both the app design and
backgrounds for various applications and mediums.

The device represents both sound waves to illustrate real life,
face to face talking and sunlight radiating or a
sunrise to represent lifting spirits, getting outside and feeling
brighter in yourself.
