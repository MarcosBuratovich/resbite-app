Marketing task list of proposal.

Establishing a strong install base is crucial for the success of any mobile
app. This comprehensive plan outlines pre-launch strategies, launch
tactics, and post-launch initiatives to drive sustainable growth and
engagement for the app.

It is strongly suggested to update this plan at the time of having a
working prototype and understanding the budget to launch the product

Pre-Launch Strategy
Market Research & Competitor Analysis
Identify target audience segments and analyze market trends and
gaps in competitor apps to position the app effectively.



Landing Page & Email List
Create a visually engaging landing page and build an email list
through exclusive content or early access offers.



Teaser Campaigns
Run social media teasers and collaborate with influencers to build
anticipation before the official launch.

Analytics & Iteration

1   User Feedback & Behavior
    Utilize analytics and user feedback tools to track engagement
    and identify potential drop-off points.



2   Campaign Optimization
    Regularly analyze paid ad data to identify high-performing
    channels and optimize campaigns.



3   Data-Driven Decisions
    Collect user feedback and run A/B tests to improve the app's
    features and user experience.
