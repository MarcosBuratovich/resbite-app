 The problem…
• The overuse and misuse of tech is
  impacting our health and wellbeing.


• Existing big social tech business
  models are based on the attention
  economy and can’t change.


• Innovation in social technology tends
  to pull people further away from
  reality i.e. antisocial




                                        3
The solution…
• Design better tech to rebalance
  people’s experience with tech.


• Design for wellness.


• Remove addictive design.


• Design for privacy.


• Design for business sustainability.

Hosted resbites
