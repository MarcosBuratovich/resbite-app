Resbite: 
Social Media Proposal
As explained in our summary Report, we all know the benefits of social media networks and how harmful they could be.
One of the main concerns is whether We should create and use a social account to promote Website.
 1. Are we going to use social media?
Yes, 
While Resbite promotes real-world connections and aims to reduce dependency on digital platforms, having a social media presence is essential for reaching and engaging with our target audience. Social media will serve as a channel to educate, raise awareness, and guide people towards using Resbite to reconnect in the real world.
2. Why?
To be where the audience is.
 While our mission is to bring people together in the real world, the first step is meeting them in the digital space where they already spend their time. Social media provides the opportunity to:
Raise Awareness:
 Social media platforms are the easiest way to educate the public about traditional social media issues and introduce Resbite as an alternative.
Drive App Adoption:
 By guiding users through an engaging digital experience, we can convert them into active Resbite users and help them transition from online to real-world interactions.
Align with Brand Values:
 Our presence will be mindful, focusing on pushing purposeful content that doesn’t contribute to the attention economy. This reflects our mission of creating healthier social technology.
3. How?
Strategically,
 Resbite will use social media in ways that align with our values:
Educational Content:
 We will share insights about the negative effects of traditional social technologies and how Resbite is designed to combat these issues. This content will focus on thought leadership in ethical tech and social wellness.
Community Building:
 Our social media will foster a sense of community through real-life success stories of people reconnecting, user-generated content
Call-to-Action (CTA) Posts:
 We will invite people to join Resbite and participate in real-world activities. This could involve challenges, events, or prompts to "disconnect to reconnect."
Influencer Partnerships:
 Collaborate with influencers focused on mental health, digital well-being, and ethical technology to amplify our message and reach.
4. When
As soon as possible
: We aim to start promoting our product immediately to establish a presence, engage with our audience, and begin building social proof. The sooner we launch our social media efforts, the quicker we can drive awareness and conversions for Resbite.
5. What do you need to achieve?
With our internal team handling the creative concepts, content approval, and graphic design, our main goal is to bring on board a skilled 
Copywriter (Native) / Social Media Manager
 to:
Develop a comprehensive 
content calendar
 proposal for organic posts, aligning with our strategic goals and creative concepts.
Write compelling copy
 tailored to resonate with our target audience, ensuring our messaging is clear, consistent, and engaging.
Publish content
 on our social media accounts, focusing on timely and strategic posts to drive engagement.
Manage the community
 to foster interaction, respond to questions, and build relationships with users as we grow our following.
6. Creative Concepts ideas
 (Work In Progress)
“Disconnect to Reconnect”
Break free from endless scrolling and rediscover authentic human connections with Resbite.
“From Likes to Life”
Swap the pursuit of virtual likes for meaningful, in-person moments that truly matter.
“Beyond the Screen”
Experience life beyond digital distractions, reconnecting with people in the real world.
“Rediscover Real”
Step away from artificial interactions and reconnect with what’s real and meaningful.
