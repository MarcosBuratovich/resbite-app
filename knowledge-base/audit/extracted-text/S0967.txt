### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide2.xml
A BACKGROUND & PURPOSE
Good afternoon, I’m Stephen Gray, CEO of Resbite
I setup my first company which was digital creative agency after I left university some 20 years ago and then I sold that to start a new business consulting on ethical design for technology. After a couple of years of speaking about what I thought were the dangers of antisocial tech and realizing it was mostly falling on deaf ears, I decided to create a new type of social technology myself with Resbite being the result.
We’re trying to create a platform that brings us together in the real world. Our mission is to be the go-to app for arranging real-world activities. Our vision for the future is to take on silicon valleys big social tech firms with a UK based ethical alternative.
We are hoping to create an app that is world changing. An app that re-unites family, friends and society. 
### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide3.xml
THE PROBLEM
The overuse and misuse of tech is impacting our health and wellbeing.
We’re basically turning to devices to conduct our conversations and relationships and therefore avoiding human interaction and real-life experiences.
To give you some examples of how this affects us: 75% of UK children now spend less time outside than prison inmates and studies on children that used smartphones for 5 or more hours a day show their levels of empathy and face-reading abilities are borderline autistic.
Existing big social tech business models are based on the attention economy.
This means they make more money; the more people stay and interact with their platform and so it forces the apps design to be unethical and create addiction. Because the problems with social tech start from the business model, existing platforms will never be able to organically change for the better.
New innovations in social technology normally pulls people further away from reality.
This is counter-intuitive to the very purpose of social. Human to human relationships are either being replaced by tech or having it placed between them. 
### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide4.xml
THE SOLUTION
We use technology to rebalance our experience of technology.
We encourage human-to-human connection and the removal of technology wherever possible and prioritize the greater good of society over user desires. We’re building a technology platform that complements natural human behavior instead of trying to influence or change it.
We design to enhance personal wellness. 
Resbite is a closed private network so there is no purpose for bots or fake profiles, no connections to trolling strangers and no confidence harming features such as like buttons.
We’re removing any addictive design. 
This includes unnecessary notifications, user tagging of photos or posts, virtual gamification and any non-stop scrolling. We want people to use our app as quickly as possible to achieve their objective and then return to real-life.
We design for privacy. 
We do not run ads, so are not dependent on detailed user data for revenue. Any data we collect is solely used to enhance the app experience.
Overall, we believe that in order to make a sustainable business we need to align our business model with helping people. The happier and healthier our customers are, the more financially successful we’ll be.
### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide5.xml
TO CREATE A RESBITE
Step 1: 
You can choose from more than 100 social activities with detailed descriptions of each one.
Step 2: 
Choose when you want the activity to take place
Step 3: 
Choose where you’d like the activity to take place
Step 4: 
Choose the number of people you’d like to attend and then invite a shortlist of your connections.
Just to clarify, the definition of a resbite is an activity idea, time and place. All user-created resbite's are free to create.
### ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide6.xml
FEATURES
Location suggestions
help you to find locations for activities for the times when you know what to do but don’t know where to go.
 
Flexible invitations
Instead of a time-specific resbite, you can opt for a connection-first resbite which creates a poll to find a time that all invitees can attend.
Guru stories
Hear the real-life stories of experts for each activity, why they love what they do and how it helped their health and wellbeing.
 
Covid
 Compliance
Optionally activate to make sure your resbite is conforming to the latest government rules and guidance for your locality.
 
Weather Checker
If you’re planning an outdoor activity, you’ll be able to check the weather forecast for an activity’s time and location.
 
Groups & Favorites
Make things easier by grouping individual connections as well as adding activities and locations to favourites.
 
Resbite Chat
Choose to chat with your resbite’s invitee list or just those that have confirmed attendance. Expires 3 days after a resbite is completed
Photo Collections
Collate photos taken by all attendees during your resbite into a central repository for download.

In the UK, the top 4 wants identified for people who had all their basic needs met were (1) to be outside, (2) to be reconnected to family and friends,(3) to take a holiday and (4) to take up a new hobby or interest. Our app caters for all of these things. These were open ended survey questions. Hardly anyone said buy a car or go to the cinema and no one said they would spend time on social media despite knowing that most probably will. These are large, highly competitive marketplaces but with not much interest according to the survey whereas were proposing to offer the top 4 wants to people and we are creating a brand-new market.
