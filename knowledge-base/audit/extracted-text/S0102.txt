Overview


The Resbite characters are centred around groups in social
scenes. Each scene or vignette illustrates the Resbite brand
ethos - ‘An app that optimises health and wellbeing by
facilitating respite fromcdigital technology, reconnecting

with our sense of self, humanity and the natural world.’


Illustration Style


The style is a combination of up-beat, lifestyle illustrations
and playful, free-flowing sketches, intended to envoke
feelings of wellbeing, full-filled and healthy lifestyles.
