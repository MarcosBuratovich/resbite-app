- **A block:** the person's messages leave the blocker's screen at once (Decision 17, challenge B3), and the team sees each block in its daily read (Decision 15, challenge I10).
- **The team:** each report pushes the moderators (Decision 10), who read the queue daily and act within 24 hours (the runbook's "The reports queue"; R-Q4's backup reader).
- **The contact address:** Data & privacy's Contact topic and a report's thanks (G-N10).

```text
Resbite is an invitation-only app for planning small get-togethers, called resbites. This version adds a chat to each resbite: the hosts and the people going write to each other, and hosts post announcements. A resbite that repeats every week is a group, and its chat stays open while it repeats.

HOW TO REACH A CHAT
Sign in with the account in Sign-In Information. On My resbites, open [title of the weekly group]. It repeats every week, so its chat is always open. On its page, tap Group chat. Another account has written messages there and posted an announcement.
The review account is also invited, and hasn't answered, to [title of the second resbite]. Its card on My resbites shows that resbite's announcement.

CHAT RULES AND FILTERING
Before their first message or announcement, everyone agrees to the chat rules: "Be kind. No harassment, hate, threats or spam. Messages that break these rules are removed, and people who break them can lose their account." The review account hasn't agreed yet, so the rules come up at its first Send.
A list of blocked terms stops a chat message or an announcement that holds one before it is stored, and the writer sees "That message can’t be sent."

REPORT AND BLOCK
- In a chat: long-press a message (or use VoiceOver's actions) for Report message. Tap a person's name above their message, or open the ••• menu and Report or block, for that person's Report and Block.
- On a resbite's list of people (People in this chat): Report and Block for each person.
- On an invitation not yet answered: long-press its card on My resbites, or tap Report or block on its resbite page (beside the announcement, and in More). They offer Report message, and Report and Block for the person who posted the announcement, the person who sent the invitation and the host.
- Profile, Connections: a connection request's ••• offers Report and Block, and a connection's ••• offers Block.
A report asks for a reason (Harassment, Spam, Not appropriate or Something else) and an optional note, then thanks the reporter and shows our contact address.
A block takes the person's messages and announcements off the blocker's screen at once, stops their notifications to the blocker and ends any connection between them. The blocked person is not told. A block is undone from the person's sheet (Unblock), or from Profile, Data & privacy, Blocked people.

HOW WE ACT ON REPORTS
Each report sends a notification to the Resbite team. We check the reports queue every day, and remove offending content and suspend the accounts responsible within 24 hours. The team also sees each block in its daily queue.

CONTACT
[the support address]. It is also shown in Profile, Data & privacy, Contact, and after every report.
```
