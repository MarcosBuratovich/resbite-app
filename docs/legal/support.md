### By the contact form

The form on this page goes straight to our support inbox. It asks for:

We use what you send only to deal with your message. We keep it for 24 months after we close your case, or longer only if the law requires it. Our [Privacy Notice](https://link.resbite.com/privacy) explains more.

## Report something in the app

Anyone in a resbite can report a message or a person. Reports go to the Resbite team. We never tell the person you reported that you reported them.

### Where to find Report

| Where you are | What to do |
|---|---|
| **In a chat** | Long-press a message (or use VoiceOver's actions) and choose **Report message**. To report a person, tap their name above their message. Or open the chat's **•••** menu, choose **Report or block**, and pick the person. |
| **On a list of people** in a resbite or group | Open the list from the chat's **•••** menu, or from the people row on the resbite's page. Each person has **Report** and **Block**. |
| **On an invitation you haven't answered** | Long-press its card on **My resbites**. Or open the resbite's page and tap **Report or block**, beside the announcement or under **More (•••)**. You can report the announcement, and report or block the person who posted it, the person who invited you and the host. |
| **In Profile → Connections** | On a connection request, tap **•••** for **Report** and **Block**. On a connection, tap **•••** for **Block** or **Remove connection**. |

### Choosing a reason

Pick the reason that fits best:

- **Illegal or dangerous**
- **Child safety**
- **Intimate image shared without consent**
- **Harassment**
- **Spam**
- **Not appropriate**
- **Something else**

Add a note if it helps (up to 500 characters), then tap **Send report**. The app thanks you and shows our address.

### What happens next

- Each new report alerts the Resbite team. We read every report every day.
- Child safety reports and intimate image reports come first.
- We aim to act on every report within 24 hours. An intimate image named in a valid report is removed within 48 hours at the latest.
- We may remove the message, suspend the account, do both, or decide that no action is needed. A message we remove reads "Message removed by Resbite" in the chat.
- We keep a copy of what you reported, even if the message or the account is later deleted. We delete a report 180 days after we close it.
- The app doesn't send you the outcome. If you want to know what happened, or you're not happy with how we dealt with your report, write to support@resbite.com. See "Complaints and appeals".

### Things you can't report directly in the app

- **A resbite's title, description or cover photo.** Report its host (the host is on the resbite's list of people) and say what's wrong in your note. Or write to us.
- **A profile photo.** Report the person.
- **A host who removed you from a resbite.** Once you've been removed, you can't report that host in the app. Write to us instead.
- **Something you can no longer see.** Write to us with what you remember.

## Report something if you don't use Resbite

You don't need an account to tell us about something on Resbite. You might be:

- someone a message or a photo is about;
- someone who saw a resbite's invitation page;
- the parent or carer of a child who is using Resbite, or who is the subject of something on it;
- someone who helps an adult to use Resbite, or who helps the adult something is about.

Under the Online Safety Act, people affected like this can report illegal content to us and use our complaints procedure, even if they don't use the service. You don't have to prove you're affected before we look at a report about illegal content.

**How to report:** email support@resbite.com, or use the contact form on this page. Tell us:

- what the problem is;
- where it is: the invitation link you saw (it starts with link.resbite.com/r/ or link.resbite.com/i/), the resbite's title and date, the name the person uses, or a description;
- how it affects you, if it does;
- how we can reach you.

We read these reports every day and deal with them in the same way and in the same times as reports made in the app.

### An intimate image of you

If a nude, sexual or other intimate image of you is on Resbite without your consent, tell us straight away. On Resbite, images appear as profile photos and as resbites' cover photos, including on invitation pages.

- **How to tell us:** email support@resbite.com with "Intimate image" in the subject, or choose "An intimate image of me" in the contact form. If you use the app, report the person and choose **Intimate image shared without consent**.
- **What to tell us:** where the image is, and that it shows you. If you're reporting for the person shown, say so, and tell us that they agree.
- **Please don't send us the image.** We'll find it from your description.
- **What we do:** we remove the image as soon as we can, and within 48 hours of your report at the latest. We suspend the account that shared it. If the same image, or one that is substantially the same, appears on Resbite again, we remove it again at once.
- **What you hear from us:** we tell you when the image has been removed.

You can also get help from:

- the **Revenge Porn Helpline**, for adults in the UK: [revengepornhelpline.org.uk](https://revengepornhelpline.org.uk);
- **StopNCII.org**, which can help stop an intimate image being shared on other sites that take part: [stopncii.org](https://stopncii.org);
- **Childline's Report Remove** tool, if the person shown is under 18: [childline.org.uk](https://www.childline.org.uk).

### Child safety

Resbite is only for adults. You must be 18 or over to use it.

- **If a child is in immediate danger, call 999.**
- **If you see sexual content involving a child, or someone trying to groom or exploit a child:** don't screenshot, save or share it. That can be a crime, even when you mean to report it. Instead, report it in the app and choose **Child safety**, or email support@resbite.com with "Child safety" in the subject and tell us where it is.
- **What we do:** we keep the evidence, report it to the National Crime Agency, take the content out of view, and ban the account. We never pass the content on to anyone else.
- You can also report concerns about a child to **CEOP**, part of the National Crime Agency: [ceop.police.uk/Safety-Centre](https://www.ceop.police.uk/Safety-Centre/).

**If you think someone using Resbite is under 18,** tell us in the same way. If we have reasonable grounds to think so, we suspend the account while we check. If we conclude the person is under 18, we close the account and delete its data.

## Block someone

You can block a person from the same places you can report them: their name in a chat, a list of people, an unanswered invitation, or Profile → Connections.

When you block someone:

- their messages and announcements disappear from your screen at once;
- they can't send you notifications;
- any connection between you ends, and unblocking won't bring it back;
- they aren't told.

If you're a host, blocking someone hides their messages from you only. To stop them for everyone, remove them from the resbite.

**To unblock someone,** open their name and tap **Unblock**, or go to **Profile → Data & privacy → Blocked people** and tap **Unblock** beside their name.

Blocks stay until you undo them. The Resbite team can see who blocked whom, so we can spot people who cause problems.

## Your data and your privacy rights

Write to **privacy@resbite.com**, or use the contact form and choose "Data protection request or complaint". You can ask us to:

- **give you a copy** of your personal data. In the app, **Profile → Data & privacy → Request a copy of my data** opens an email to us, ready to send;
- **correct** something that's wrong. You can change your name and photo yourself in Profile. To change the phone number or email address you sign in with, write to privacy@resbite.com;
- **delete** your data (see "Delete your account" above);
- **stop or limit** how we use your data, or **object** to it;
- **give your data to you in a form you can take elsewhere**.

Some of these rights apply only in certain situations. Our [Privacy Notice](https://link.resbite.com/privacy) explains when.

Where we rely on a permission you gave on your iPhone, such as notifications or access to your contacts, you can withdraw it at any time in your iPhone's **Settings → Resbite**.

**If someone invited you to a resbite by your phone number** and you don't want Resbite to use your number, write to privacy@resbite.com with the number. We delete it from invitations. We also add a scrambled form of the number to a "do not invite" list, so nobody can invite that number through Resbite again until you tell us otherwise.

**What happens next:**

- We may ask you to show that the account or the number is yours. We'll ask only for what we need to be sure, and we'll never ask for your password.
- We answer within one month. If a request is complicated, or you send several, we may need up to two more months. If so, we tell you why within the first month.
- We don't usually charge for this.

Our [Privacy Notice](https://link.resbite.com/privacy) explains what we hold, why, who helps us run Resbite, and how long we keep things.

## Complaints and appeals

You can complain to us about anything to do with Resbite. This section explains each kind of complaint, what we do with it and how long it takes. Everyone can use it: people who use Resbite, and people affected by something on Resbite who don't.

### Complaints about how we handle personal data

Send your complaint to **privacy@resbite.com**, use the contact form ("Data protection request or complaint"), or write to us by post. Tell us what happened, what you'd like us to do, and how to reach you. If it's about an account, tell us the phone number or email address it uses.

- **We acknowledge your complaint within 30 days of receiving it**, as the law requires. In practice we aim to do it within 5 working days.
- We look into it without undue delay. We may ask you questions, and we keep you told about our progress.
- We tell you the outcome in writing: what we found, what we've done, and what you can do if you're still unhappy.

You also have the right to complain to **the Information Commission (ICO)**, the UK's data protection regulator, at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk). The ICO will usually expect you to have complained to us first.

### Complaints about content, reports and our safety measures

Write to **support@resbite.com**, use the contact form ("Complaint"), or write to us by post. You can complain if:

- you've seen something on Resbite that you think is illegal or harmful (this is the same as a report; see above);
- you reported something and think we didn't deal with it properly or quickly enough;
- you think our reporting doesn't work, is hard to use, or isn't working for you because of a disability;
- you think we're not meeting our duties under the Online Safety Act, including our duty to protect your freedom of expression and your privacy when we keep Resbite safe.

**What happens:** we acknowledge your complaint and tell you roughly how long we'll take. We look into it, decide what to do, and tell you the outcome and why. If you've told us about content that may be illegal, we treat your complaint as a report and review the content within 24 hours.

### Appeals: if we removed your content or suspended your account

If you think we were wrong to remove something you posted, to suspend your account, or to block your message with our word filter, you can appeal.

- **How:** write to support@resbite.com with "Appeal" in the subject, or use the contact form ("Appeal"). Tell us which account it's about (the phone number or email address it uses), what we did, and why you think it was wrong. You can do this even if you can no longer sign in.
- **Deadline:** there isn't one. But it helps to appeal soon, while we still have the details. We delete closed reports after 180 days.
- **Who decides:** where we can, someone other than the person who made the first decision looks at your appeal.
- **Time:** we acknowledge your appeal within 2 working days and decide it within 10 working days. If it will take longer, we tell you why.
- **If we agree with you:** we lift any suspension straight away, and we put right what we can. A message we removed can't be put back in the chat, but you're free to send it again.
- **If we don't agree:** we tell you why, and what you can do next.

**About our word filter.** Before anything is saved, Resbite checks chat messages and announcements, resbites' titles, descriptions, notes to guests and meeting details, and the names people use, against a short list of blocked words. A message that contains one isn't sent, and you see "That message can't be sent." Other text that contains one isn't saved. Our [Terms of Use](https://link.resbite.com/terms) explain how the filter works. If you think the filter stopped a message it shouldn't have, or is being used in a way our Terms don't allow, you can complain or appeal as above. If you believe we've broken our Terms with you, you may also have the right to take the matter to court.

### If your account is suspended

If we suspend your account, the app shows "This account can't use Resbite at the moment" when you open it. The same screen shows our support address and a **Delete account** button.

- If your account has an email address, we email you the reason when we suspend it. If it doesn't, write to us and we'll tell you why.
- You can appeal at any time (see above).
- You can still delete your account. If you can sign in, use **Delete account** on that screen. If you can't sign in, email privacy@resbite.com.
- While a host's account is suspended, their weekly groups pause.

### If you're still not happy

- Ask us to look again. Reply to our answer and tell us why you disagree.
- For data protection, you can complain to **the Information Commission (ICO)** at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).
- For online safety, you can tell **Ofcom** at [ofcom.org.uk](https://www.ofcom.org.uk). Ofcom doesn't decide individual complaints, but it uses what people tell it to check that services like ours protect their users.
- You may also be able to go to court.

## Legal complaints about content: defamation, copyright and privacy

If you think something on Resbite breaks the law about you or your rights, write to **support@resbite.com** with "Legal complaint" in the subject, use the contact form ("Legal complaint"), or write to us by post. We may remove content sooner than the steps below if it clearly breaks our Terms or the law.

On Resbite, people can post chat messages and announcements, a resbite's title, description, cover photo and notes, and their own name and profile photo. When you tell us where something is, please give the resbite's title and date or its invitation link, the name the person uses, and the date and time of the message if you know them.

### Defamation

If you think something on Resbite is defamatory of you, send us a **notice of complaint**. It must include:

1. your name, and an email address where we can reach you;
2. where on Resbite the statement is (see above);
3. what the statement says, and why it is defamatory of you;
4. what you take the statement to mean;
5. which parts you believe are factually wrong, or are opinions not supported by facts;
6. that you don't have enough information about the person who posted it to bring legal proceedings against them;
7. whether you agree to us giving your name and email address to that person.

We follow the steps in the Defamation (Operators of Websites) Regulations 2013. In this section, "48 hours" doesn't count Saturdays, Sundays, Good Friday, Christmas Day or bank holidays in England and Wales.

| When | What we do |
|---|---|
| Within 48 hours of receiving your notice | If your notice is missing something, we tell you in writing and explain what a notice must contain, so you can send it again. If it's complete, we contact the person who posted the statement, privately and in writing, and tell you in writing that we've done so. If we can't contact them, we remove the statement and tell you. |
| What we send the poster | A copy of your notice (without your name and email address if you asked us to leave them out). A warning that the statement may be removed unless they reply in writing by midnight (UK time) at the end of the fifth day after the day we contacted them. That reply must say whether they want the statement removed and, if not, give their full name and postal address and say whether they agree to us passing those details to you. |
| If the poster doesn't reply in time | We remove the statement within 48 hours of the deadline and tell you. |
| If the poster agrees to removal | We remove the statement within 48 hours of their reply and tell you. |
| If the poster replies but leaves out what we asked for, or gives a name or address that is obviously false | We remove the statement within 48 hours of their reply and tell you. |
| If the poster wants the statement to stay and agrees to us passing on their details | Within 48 hours of their reply, we tell you that they want it to stay, that it hasn't been removed, and give you their name and postal address. |
| If the poster wants the statement to stay and doesn't agree to us passing on their details | Within 48 hours of their reply, we tell you that they want it to stay, that it hasn't been removed, and that they haven't agreed to us giving you their details. We give them to you only if a court orders us to. |
| If the same person posts the same thing about you again | Send us a new notice and tell us about your earlier ones. If you've already sent us notices about it on two or more earlier occasions, we remove the repost within 48 hours of your notice. |

### Copyright and other intellectual property

If something on Resbite uses your copyright work, trade mark or other intellectual property without permission, tell us:

- your name and how to contact you;
- the work or right concerned, and that you own it or are authorised to act for the owner;
- what the content is and where it is on Resbite;
- that you believe in good faith that the use isn't authorised, and that the information you've given is accurate.

We look at it promptly, remove or disable the content if your complaint is well founded, and tell the person who posted it. They can reply, and if they show they were entitled to use it, we may restore it. We may close the accounts of people who keep infringing other people's rights.

### Privacy

If someone has posted your personal information on Resbite without your agreement, for example your phone number, your address, or a photo of you, tell us where it is and why it worries you. We decide whether it breaks the law or our Terms, and remove it if it does. If it puts you at risk, say so: we treat it as a safety report and act within 24 hours. For an intimate image of you, see "An intimate image of you" above.

### Courts, police and regulators

Court orders, police requests and requests from regulators should be sent to privacy@resbite.com with "Legal request" in the subject, or to our registered office by post. If there is a risk to someone's life, say so in the subject line. We check every request and its legal basis, and we disclose only what the law requires or allows.

## How you hear the outcome

- **If you wrote to us,** by email or the form, we reply by email to the address you gave. If you wrote by post and asked for a reply by post, we reply by post.
- **Our answer says** what we decided and why, what we've done, and what you can do next.
- **If you'd rather not hear from us** while we look into a report or complaint, say so. We'll then send only the outcome, or nothing more if you ask.
- **If you reported something in the app,** the app doesn't send you the outcome. You'll often see the result, for example a removed message. If you'd like to know more, write to support@resbite.com.
- **If we removed your content or suspended your account,** see "If your account is suspended" above.
- **We keep your identity private.** We never tell a person who reported them, including if that person asks us for a copy of their data.
