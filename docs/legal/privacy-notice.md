This is our Privacy Policy. It explains what personal data Resbite uses, why, who else is involved, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It is written for everyone Resbite touches: people who use the app, people who answer an invitation on our website, people who are invited by their phone number but don't use Resbite, and people who contact us.

You can find it in the app (on the welcome and sign-in screens, and in **Profile → Data & privacy → About and legal**) and at https://link.resbite.com/privacy.

---

## The short version

- **Resbite Ltd** runs Resbite and decides how your data is used.
- **You need a verified mobile number** to use Resbite. Friends find you and invite you by it.
- **What you add to a resbite is seen by the people in it.** The exact meeting place is shown to everyone invited, before they answer.
- **Chat is stored on our servers and is not end-to-end encrypted.** People who join a chat later can read earlier messages.
- **Anyone who has your number can see that you're on Resbite**, and invite you.
- **Notifications pass through Expo in the United States.** A chat notification shows the sender's first name and the first 120 characters of the message.
- **Invited by number but not on Resbite?** Read section 10. You can object at any time, and we'll stop anyone inviting your number.
- **No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.**
- **Delete your account in the app at any time:** Profile → Data & privacy → Delete account. We finish within 30 days.
- **Questions, requests or complaints:** privacy@resbite.com. We answer within one month.

## 3. What we collect, and why

Most of what we hold, you give us. Some comes from other people who use Resbite: for example, when a host invites you, or someone reports a message. Some is created automatically, such as records that keep your account secure. Section 4 gives the lawful basis for each use, and section 7 says how long we keep each item.

### 3.1 Your account and signing in

**Your mobile number (required).** Every account needs a mobile number that you verify with a code we send by text. We use it to:

- sign you in;
- let friends find you and invite you by your number (section 3.5);
- match invitations sent to your number to your account;
- keep to one account per number;
- send you a service text if your account has no email address and we need to reach you: for example, to tell you we suspended your account, to check it's you when you ask us for something, or to warn you about a security problem. We never send marketing texts.

Our SMS provider, Twilio, sends the codes and these texts. If you don't give a verified number, you can't use Resbite. We never show your number to other users. People who already have it, such as a friend who invited you by it, of course know it.

**Other ways to sign in (optional).** You can also sign in with:

- **an email address and password.** We store your password only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody, including us, can read it. We send confirmation, sign-in code and password reset emails through Resend;
- **Sign in with Apple.** Apple gives us an Apple user ID, an email address and, the first time, your name. If you choose Hide My Email, the address is an Apple relay address that forwards to you;
- **Sign in with Google.** Google gives us a Google user ID, your email address, your name and a link to your Google profile picture. We don't use the picture.

**Your name.** The name you choose, between 1 and 80 characters. People you plan with see it. If you host, anyone with your event link sees it (section 3.3).

**Your account ID.** A random code that links your records together. It says nothing about you.

**Sign-in records.** While you are signed in, our sign-in service keeps a session: the tokens that keep you signed in, your IP address, your device or browser type, and the times. We use them to keep you signed in and to protect your account.

**Your age and Terms record.** Every account confirms that its holder is 18 or over and accepts our Terms of Use. We record that you did, which version of the Terms you accepted, when and how. When we publish a new version of the Terms that needs your agreement, we ask you again and record your answer. Before you send your first chat message, we record that you accepted the chat rules, and which version.

**When you used the app.** We record the first and the latest time you used the app. We show you to friends as "On Resbite" only after you have used the app.

**Details we no longer collect.** Test versions of Resbite before version 1.0 let people add an optional date of birth, a second phone number, a city and interests. Version 1.0 doesn't collect them, and we have deleted the ones we held.

### 3.2 Your profile photo

A profile photo is optional. You choose it with your iPhone's own photo picker, so Resbite can't see the rest of your photo library. Before it is uploaded, the app resizes it and removes the hidden details photos carry, such as where and with which camera it was taken.

We store it privately in London. People who can see you in a resbite, your connections, and people you have a connection request with can see it, through links that stop working after five minutes. If you change or remove your photo, we delete the old file within 7 days.

### 3.3 Resbites and weekly groups

A resbite is a plan someone hosts. A weekly group is a resbite that repeats, every week or every two weeks. In this policy, each time a weekly group meets is a "week".

**What we hold:** the title, description and categories; the time and time zone; whether it repeats; a rough area; the exact meeting place; meeting details (such as a door code or directions); the note to guests; a cover photo and its description; the guest list, with each person's answer and who brought whom; co-hosts; requests to join from an event link; who muted a resbite; who left a weekly group; and the resbite's links.

**Why:** so you can plan, invite people, answer, and run the resbite.

**Who sees what:**

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| Anyone with the event link, on the web, without signing in | The host's name, the title, the time, the rough area, whether the place is decided, whether it repeats, how many are going, and the cover photo |
| Everyone invited, even before they answer | All of the above, plus the description, the exact meeting place and the note to guests |
| The hosts, and guests who answered Going or Maybe | Also the meeting details |
| The hosts | The guest list with every answer, who brought whom, and requests to join |
| Guests who have answered, and in a weekly group everyone in its chat | The guest list, with everyone's answers |

**Think before you type a home address.** The exact meeting place is shown to everyone invited, before they answer.

**On your phone.** If you tap the meeting place, Apple Maps opens and Apple receives the place you tapped. If you add a resbite to your calendar, your iPhone's own calendar form opens; Resbite doesn't read your calendar. Reminders are set on your phone by iOS and stay there.

### 3.4 Chat

Every resbite has a chat.

**What we hold:** messages and announcements (up to 1,000 characters each), who wrote them and when; short lines Resbite adds, such as who joined or answered; and how far you have read.

**Who can read a chat:**

- in a one-off resbite: the hosts and the guests who answered Going or Maybe;
- in a weekly group: the hosts and everyone on the current week's guest list, whatever their answer, including guests who haven't answered yet. They can read the group's whole chat history;
- **people who join later can read the whole chat, including messages sent before they joined.**

**Chat is not end-to-end encrypted.** We store messages on our servers so that the people in the chat can read them. Like everything on our servers, our team can reach them. We don't read chats as a matter of course. We look at a message when someone reports it to us, or when the law requires us to.

A one-off resbite's chat becomes read-only 48 hours after the resbite ends.

If you delete a message, its words disappear for everyone. A host can remove a message too. A note that a message was deleted or removed stays in the chat.

**Blocked words.** Before a message or announcement is sent, we check it against a short list of words and phrases that aren't allowed. If it contains one, it isn't sent and we don't keep it. You can change the words and try again. The same check runs on titles, descriptions, notes to guests, meeting details and names. See section 13.

### 3.5 Finding friends, and being found

**When you invite people from your contacts:**

- The app reads your contacts only when you choose to invite from them, and only on your phone. It reads names and phone numbers. iOS lets you share only some of your contacts if you prefer.
- **Your address book is never uploaded.** Contact names never leave your phone.
- We receive only the numbers you pick, and we check which of them belong to people on Resbite. We don't keep that check. We keep only a count of how many numbers you checked that day, because each person can check up to 500 numbers a day.
- If a number belongs to someone on Resbite, we add them to your guest list and tell them. If it doesn't, see section 3.6.
- You can type a number instead of using your contacts.

**Being found:**

- **Anyone on Resbite who has your number can see that you're "On Resbite", and can invite you straight to a resbite.** You'll get the invitation and can answer it or leave it.
- This is how Resbite works, so there is no setting to hide it. People you have blocked can't find you this way. If you don't want someone to find you, block them. You can also object (section 11) or delete your account (section 8).
- You show as "On Resbite" only after you have used the app.

### 3.6 Inviting people who aren't on Resbite

When you invite a number that isn't on Resbite, we keep:

- the number and its country code;
- which resbite it is for, and that you invited them;
- a scrambled copy of the code in their personal link;
- whether and when they answered;
- how many times someone tried to confirm a number on that link (we allow 10 tries an hour).

Your phone keeps the link and the contact's name. We never receive the name.

**You send the invitation yourself,** from WhatsApp or Messages, with text you can see and change first. We don't send it and don't see it. Please invite only people you know.

If you remove someone from a weekly group, or withdraw their invitation, we may keep their number on that group's removal list, so another guest can't bring them straight back.

The people you invite have their own rights, set out in section 10. If one of them objects, we stop anyone inviting their number again.

### 3.7 Connections

People who have shared a resbite can connect. One asks, the other accepts.

- We keep who you're connected to, who asked, the resbite where you met, and when.
- Only the two of you see your connection. People who are connected can see each other's name and photo, and can invite each other straight to a resbite.
- **"Not now" and removals.** If you answer a request with "Not now", or remove a connection, we keep a record of it for the resbite where you met. **The other person isn't told.** They can't ask you again from that resbite, though they can from another one you share. We keep this record for 12 months.
- A request nobody answers is deleted after 90 days.

### 3.8 Notifications

Notifications are optional. Resbite works without them.

**What we hold:** if you allow notifications, the app registers a push token for your phone through Expo. We store the token with your device type and the time.

**What we send:** invitations, answers, changes, cancellations, notes to guests, requests to join and approvals, co-host appointments, connection requests and acceptances, chat messages and announcements. **We never send marketing notifications.**

**What a notification shows:** the resbite's title and time, the name of the person who did something, and what happened. **A chat notification shows the sender's first name and the first 120 characters of the message.** A notification never shows the meeting place, the meeting details, the description or the text of the note to guests.

**How it travels:** from our servers to the Expo push service in the United States, then to Apple's push service, then to your phone. Expo deletes the content once it has passed it to Apple, and keeps delivery receipts for 24 hours.

**Your lock screen.** Anyone who can see your phone may see your notifications. You can:

- hide what they say: iPhone **Settings → Notifications → Resbite → Show Previews**;
- mute a resbite or a chat in the app;
- turn notifications off for Resbite in iPhone Settings.

People on our moderation team get a notification that says only "A new report is waiting." It contains nothing from the report.

### 3.9 Safety: reports, blocks and suspensions

**Reports.** When you report a message or a person, we keep:

- who made the report, and who or what it is about;
- the reason you chose: Illegal or dangerous, Child safety, Intimate image shared without consent, Harassment, Spam, Not appropriate, or Something else;
- any details you add (up to 500 characters);
- a copy of the reported words and who wrote them, so we can act even if the message is deleted;
- our notes, and what we did.

If you delete your account, a report you made stays until we close it, and then for 180 days, but we remove your account from it (section 7).

Our moderation team reads reports every day. It is small: two named people who work under our written instructions and a duty of confidentiality. **We don't tell the person you reported who reported them.**

People who don't use Resbite can report something to us, including an intimate image of themselves, by email or with the form at https://link.resbite.com/support. We deal with reports of intimate images shared without consent within 48 hours, and we suspend the account that shared the image at once.

**Allegations of crimes.** Reports can contain allegations that someone committed a crime, such as harassment or threats. The law calls this criminal offence data. We use it only to deal with the report, keep people safe and meet our legal duties, under [[OWNER: the DPA 2018 Schedule 1 condition for criminal offence data in reports, chosen with the solicitor — proposed: paragraph 12 (regulatory requirements relating to unlawful acts), with paragraph 10 (preventing or detecting unlawful acts) as the alternative, each extended to criminal offence data by paragraph 36; paragraph 18 (safeguarding) for child-safety reports; paragraph 33 (legal claims) for records under a legal hold]] of the Data Protection Act 2018.

**Blocks.** If you block someone, we keep who blocked whom, and when. The person you blocked isn't told. Our team can see blocks, so we can spot people who cause problems.

**Suspensions.** If we suspend an account, we keep a record of which account, the report it followed, what we changed and when. If a suspended account is deleted, we remove the account from the record and keep only the reason and the dates (section 7). We tell the person how to appeal. Our Terms of Use explain when we suspend accounts.

**When the law requires it** we pass information to the police, the National Crime Agency or Ofcom. Section 5.4 explains.

### 3.10 When you contact us

If you email us or use our contact form, we keep what you send, your contact details, and our replies. The contact form asks for your name, an email address we can reply to, the topic and the details. To stop the form being misused, its server also uses technical data such as your IP address. We use all of this to deal with your message, and records of data requests and complaints to show that we handled them properly.

Our emails, and our records of requests and complaints, are kept with [[OWNER: mailbox provider for support@resbite.com and privacy@resbite.com — proposed: a business email service under a signed data processing agreement, with data held in the UK or EU and MFA on]].

### 3.11 Security, logs and backups

**Logs.** Our hosting services keep logs of requests to our servers: the time, what was asked for, your account ID and technical details such as your IP address and device or browser type. We use them to run Resbite, fix faults and protect it from abuse. Our main service, Supabase, keeps logs for 7 days, including a log of sign-in events. Vercel, which hosts our website, keeps request logs for the period our plan with it sets: [[OWNER: Vercel plan for the link site, which sets how long its request logs last — proposed: Pro, which keeps request logs for 1 day and is covered by Vercel's data processing agreement]].

**Limits.** To stop abuse, we count things such as code requests, numbers checked (500 a day) and tries on an invitation link (10 an hour).

**Bot check.** When you ask for a sign-in code, in the app or on our website, a check by [[OWNER: CAPTCHA vendor — proposed: Cloudflare Turnstile or hCaptcha]] makes sure you are a person and not a program. It looks at technical signals from your phone or browser, such as your IP address and browser details. The vendor receives these signals to run the check for us. We don't use them for anything else. If the vendor also uses the signals to improve its own bot detection, it does that as a separate controller, under its own privacy policy.

**Backups.** We back up our database every day and keep each backup for 7 days, in London. Photos are not in these backups. If we ever restore a backup, we first repeat every account deletion made since it was taken.

**Deletion receipts.** When you delete your account, we keep a short receipt of the request until 30 days after the deletion is complete, to prove it was done and to repeat it if we restore a backup.

### 3.12 Our website, link.resbite.com

Our website shows invitation pages for people who don't have the app, and our legal and support pages.

- **Event links** (`/r/`) show the public details of a resbite (section 3.3). Anyone with the link can ask to join.
- **Personal invitation links** (`/i/`) are for one invited number. The page shows the last two digits of the number invited.

If you answer, or ask to join, on the website, you give us your mobile number (on a personal link we check it is the number invited, and keep only a count of tries), the code we text you, your name and your answer. **Confirming your number creates a simple Resbite account** that holds them. The hosts see your name and answer, and so do the other guests who have answered. If you later add the same number in the app, we move your answers into your app account and delete the website account. If a website account has no resbite for 12 months, we delete it. If you ask for a code but never confirm your number, we delete the number within 30 days.

What the website stores in your browser is in section 9.

### 3.13 Usage records

- **Link opens.** We count how often each event link is opened in the app. We record the link and the time, not who opened it.
- **App use.** The first and latest time you used the app (section 3.1).
- **Apple's statistics.** If you turned on **Share With App Developers** in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics about the app. Apple prepares them, and they don't include your Resbite account details. You can turn this off in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements**.

### 3.14 What we don't do

- We don't sell your data, or share it for advertising.
- We don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
- We don't use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools in the app or on the website.
- We don't use your location. The app doesn't ask for it.
- We don't collect health or fitness data.
- We don't use your data to train AI, and we don't send it to AI services.
- We don't ask for sensitive information, such as your health, religion or sexuality. If you choose to share it in a chat or a report, we store and show it only as part of that chat or report.

Resbite is free. If we add paid features in a later version, we'll update this policy before we use any payment information.

## 5. Who we share it with

### 5.1 Other people on Resbite

What other people see depends on what you do. In short:

- people in a resbite with you see your name, your photo and your answer, and read what you write in its chat (sections 3.3 and 3.4);
- anyone with an event link sees a host's name (section 3.3);
- anyone with your number can see you're "On Resbite" (section 3.5);
- your connections see your name and photo (section 3.7).

Nobody on Resbite sees your phone number, your email or who you blocked or reported.

### 5.2 Service providers

These companies run parts of Resbite for us. They may use your data only on our instructions, under a contract that requires them to protect it.

| Provider | What they do for us | What they receive | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore) | Our database, sign-in service, file storage, server functions, logs and backups | Everything in section 3 that is stored on our servers | Stored in London, UK. Supabase and its own providers may reach it from outside the UK to run and support the service |

## 8. Deleting your account

### In the app

Go to **Profile → Data & privacy → Delete account**. You prove it's you with a new code sent to your number, your password, a code sent to your email, or Sign in with Apple. This works for every kind of account, including a suspended one.

**We finish within 30 days, and usually much sooner.** We confirm when the deletion is complete.

### What deletion removes

- your number, email, sign-in identities and password;
- your name and photo. Where you appeared, you are shown as "Deleted member";
- your places on guest lists and your answers;
- on every resbite you host, past and future: its title, description, cover photo, note to guests, meeting details, area and meeting place;
- your connections and "Not now" records, blocks you made and blocks of you, your chat rules record, your push tokens and your notifications;
- your account's link to the reports you made;
- **the words of your chat messages and announcements.** They show as deleted, with no author;
- if you signed in with Apple, Resbite's access to your Apple account. We ask Apple to revoke it.

### If you host

- Future resbites you host are cancelled, and your guests are told.
- A weekly group you host ends, even if it has co-hosts.
- Past resbites you hosted stay in your guests' history as a blank record: no title, description, cover photo, note to guests, meeting details, area, meeting place or host name. We delete them 12 months after they ended.

### What we keep after deletion

- Reports about you, with the copy of what was reported, until we close them and then 180 days. This lets us keep other people safe.
- Reports you made, until we close them and then 180 days, without your account. This lets us finish dealing with them.
- If your account was suspended, a record of the suspension's reason and dates, without your account, for 12 months after the deletion (section 7).
- Messages other people sent. They belong to the people who wrote them.
- A deletion receipt, for 30 days after the deletion is complete.
- Records under a legal hold (section 7).

Your data can stay in our backups and logs for up to 7 days after it is deleted. If we restore a backup, we repeat your deletion first.

### Without the app

- **Website-only accounts.** If you answered on our website and never used the app, email privacy@resbite.com, or use the form at https://link.resbite.com/support. Tell us the number. We'll check that it's yours and delete the account within 30 days. If you don't ask, we delete a website-only account once it has had no resbite for 12 months.
- **If you can't sign in**, for example because your account was banned, email privacy@resbite.com in the same way.

## 9. Storage on your phone and in your browser

The law (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, regulation 6) says a service must ask you before it stores information on your phone or computer, or reads it, unless an exception applies. One exception is storage that is strictly necessary to give you something you asked for. Everything Resbite stores is covered by that exception, so we don't need to ask. **We use no cookies, no advertising or analytics storage, and no tracking identifiers.**

### In the app

The app stores these on your iPhone, mostly in the iOS Keychain:

- your sign-in session, which includes your number and email;
- your people lists and groups, with contact names and numbers;
- the personal invitation links you made, with each contact's name and number;
- places you used recently;
- messages waiting to be sent;
- your reminder settings, and which resbites you added to your calendar;
- a photo or cover waiting to upload;
- an email address waiting to be confirmed;
- an invitation link, and your 18+ tick, waiting for you to finish signing in;
- your push token;
- a resbite you are creating that hasn't reached our servers yet;
- after you ask to delete your account, a receipt for the request.

**Signing out clears almost all of it,** and removes Resbite's notifications from your phone. Two items stay after you sign out: a resbite you were creating that hasn't been saved yet, so you don't lose it (it goes when it is saved, or when you delete your account), and an email address waiting to be confirmed (it goes when you confirm it or choose to forget it).

**Deleting the app may not delete the Keychain.** iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so they may still be there if you install Resbite again on the same phone. To remove them, sign out, or delete your account, before you delete the app.

Signing out doesn't stop links you have already shared from working. Withdraw them from the resbite first if you want them to stop.

The app reads your contacts only when you choose to invite from them, and your photos only through the iPhone photo picker. It doesn't ask for your location.

### On link.resbite.com

- No cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, except the bot check below.
- **Your session on an invitation page.** When you confirm your number on an event link or a personal invitation link, the page keeps your session in your browser's local storage: your sign-in tokens, the number you confirmed, and when the resbite starts. There is one entry for each link. It lets you see your answer if you come back. It is removed when the resbite starts, when you tap **Not you? Sign out**, or if it can't be read.
- **The bot check.** When you ask for a code, [[OWNER: CAPTCHA vendor — proposed: Cloudflare Turnstile or hCaptcha]] may store or read information in your browser, or in the app, to tell people from programs. This is strictly necessary to protect sign-in.

If we ever want to add analytics, we'll either ask you first or meet the law's conditions, and we'll update this policy before we start.

## 10. If someone invited you by your number

This section is for you if a friend who uses Resbite invited you to a resbite using your mobile number, and you don't have a Resbite account.

**Who is using your number.** Resbite Ltd (section 1).

**Where we got it.** From the friend who invited you, the host. They picked it from their phone's contacts or typed it in. We didn't get it from any public source.

**What we hold:**

- your mobile number and its country code;
- which resbite you were invited to, and who invited you;
- a scrambled copy of the code in your personal link;
- whether and when you answered;
- how many times someone tried to confirm a number on your link (we allow 10 tries an hour).

We don't receive your name. It stays on the host's phone. If a host removed you from a weekly group, your number may also be on that group's removal list. When the host first picked your number, we checked whether it belonged to someone on Resbite. We didn't keep that check.

**Why we use it, and our lawful basis.** To deliver the host's invitation, let you answer it, and check that the person answering holds the number that was invited. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: the host's interest in inviting you, and yours in receiving the invitation and answering it. We have weighed these against your privacy. We use your number for nothing else. We don't message you, unless you ask for a code on the invitation page, or ask us to check that the number is yours. We show it to nobody except the host who gave it to us. And we delete it quickly. You can ask us for our assessment.

**How the invitation reaches you.** The host sends it from their own phone, through WhatsApp or Messages. We don't send it and we don't see it.

**Who sees your number.** The host who invited you already has it. Other hosts of the resbite, and the invitation page, show only its last two digits. Supabase stores it for us in London (sections 5.2 and 6). If you ask for a code, our SMS provider, Twilio, receives your number to send it. We don't share it with anyone else.

**How long we keep it.** Until you answer or the invitation is withdrawn, or 30 days after the resbite ends, whichever comes first. On a removal list: 30 days after a one-off resbite ends, or 90 days after a weekly group's last week.

**If you answer.** Confirming your number on the invitation page creates a simple Resbite account with your number, your name and your answer. The rest of this policy then applies to you. You can ask us to delete that account at any time (section 8).

> ### Your right to object
>
> **You can object at any time to our use of your number. You don't have to give a reason.**
>
> Email **privacy@resbite.com**, or use the form at https://link.resbite.com/support, and tell us the number. We may first text a code to that number, to check that it's yours.
>
> We will then:
>
> - delete your number from every invitation and removal list that holds it; and
> - add it to our **do-not-invite list**, so that nobody on Resbite can invite you by your number again.
>
> The do-not-invite list doesn't hold your number in readable form. It holds a code made from your number with a secret key. We can use the code only to recognise your number when someone tries to invite it. We keep it until you tell us you want to be invitable again.

**Your other rights.** You can also ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to limit how we use it (section 11). You can complain to us and to the ICO (section 12).
