You can delete your Resbite account at any time, whichever way you sign in. This page explains how, what we delete, what we keep and for how long, and what happens to the things you shared with other people.

**Deleting the app does not delete your account.** If you remove Resbite from your iPhone, your account and everything in it stay on our servers. To delete your account, follow the steps below before you remove the app.

**Signing out does not delete your account either.**

---

## The short version

- In the app: **Profile → Data & privacy → Delete account**.
- You prove it's you, type DELETE and confirm.
- Your access ends at once, on every device.
- We finish deleting your account within 30 days. Usually it takes a few minutes.
- You can check when it's complete in the app on the same iPhone.
- If you can't use the app, email **privacy@resbite.com**.
- Once your request is accepted, it can't be undone.

---

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open Resbite and tap **Profile**.
2. Tap **Data & privacy**.
3. Tap **Delete account**, the red row at the bottom.
4. If you like, tap **What happens** to read what will be deleted.
5. Under **Confirm it's you**, prove that the account is yours. How you do this depends on how you sign in (see the table below).
6. Under **Type DELETE**, type the word DELETE.
7. Tap **Permanently delete account**. If you sign in with Apple, tap **Continue with Apple** instead: it confirms it's you and deletes the account in one step.

The app tells you that your request has been accepted, clears what Resbite saved on your iPhone and signs you out.

### How you prove it's you

| How you sign in | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Your phone number and a code** | We text a new code to your verified phone number. Enter it. |
| **Email and password** | We email you a new code. Enter it. Or tap **Use my password instead** and enter your password. |
| **Google** | We email a new code to the email address of your Google account. Enter it. |
| **Apple** | Type DELETE, then tap **Continue with Apple** and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. |

If you sign in in more than one way, the app shows you which proof to use.

### Why we ask you to prove it's you again

Being signed in isn't enough. Deleting an account can't be undone, so we ask for a fresh proof, made in the last few minutes: a new code, your password, or a new Apple sign-in. This stops someone who picks up your unlocked phone from deleting your account. We never ask for your password by email.

### If your account is suspended

If we have suspended your account but you can still sign in, the app shows "This account can't use Resbite at the moment". Tap **Delete account** on that screen and follow the same steps.

If you can't sign in at all, for example because your account has been banned, ask us by email (see below).

### If you've lost your iPhone

Install Resbite on another iPhone, sign in, and follow the steps above. Or ask us by email.

## Ask us to delete your account by email

Use this if:

- you answered an invitation on our website (link.resbite.com) and never used the app, so you have a web-only account; or
- you can't sign in, for example because your account has been banned; or
- you can't use the app for any other reason.

**How:** email **privacy@resbite.com** with "Delete my account" in the subject, or use the contact form on our [Support page](https://link.resbite.com/support) and choose "Delete my web-only or banned account". Tell us:

- the phone number you used with Resbite, and the email address too if your account has one;
- that you want your account deleted.

**What happens next:**

1. We reply within 2 working days.
2. We check that the request comes from the person who holds the account, for example by sending a one-time code to the account's verified phone number or email address and asking you to tell us the code. We ask only for what we need, and we never ask for your password.
3. We delete the account in the same way as the app does, within 30 days of confirming it's you.
4. We email you to confirm when deletion is complete.

If you don't ask, we delete a web-only account ourselves once it has had no resbite for 12 months. If you asked for a code on our website but never confirmed your number, we delete the number within 30 days.

If you used the website on a shared computer, you can also clear Resbite's data from that browser. The website keeps your answer session there only until the resbite starts.

## How long it takes, and how you know it's done

- **Straight away:** your access ends on every device. Nobody can use your account any more, and the changes in "Deleted straight away" below happen at once.
- **Within 30 days:** we finish deleting everything in "Deleted while we finish". This usually takes a few minutes.
- **Confirmation:** open Resbite on the iPhone you used. The welcome screen shows **Check account deletion**. Tap it, then **Check deletion status**. It tells you when deletion is complete. You can check for 30 days after it completes. If you asked by email, we email you instead.
- **Backups:** our daily backups are kept for 7 days. Your data leaves them within 7 days after deletion is complete. If we ever have to restore a backup, we delete your account again before the service goes back online.

## What we delete

### Deleted straight away

| What | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Your access** | You're signed out everywhere. Your account can no longer read or change anything. |
| **Your name and photo** | Other people see "Deleted member" instead of your name. Your profile photo disappears. |
| **Your messages and announcements** | Their words are removed, and they show as "Message deleted". Where a chat names you in a line such as who joined, it shows "Someone". A pinned announcement of yours is unpinned. |
| **Resbites you host that haven't happened yet** | They are cancelled. Every guest gets a notification: "This resbite was cancelled." |
| **Details of every resbite you host** | On all of them, past and future, we remove the title (it reads "Resbite"), the description, the cover photo, notes to guests, meeting details and the area. The meeting place reads "Meeting place removed". |
| **Resbites and groups you joined** | You leave them all. People you brought along stay on the guest list. If you co-host someone else's resbite, you stop being a co-host when we finish (see below). |
| **Connections** | All your connections and connection requests end, in both directions. So do "Not now" answers and removals between you and other people. |
| **Blocks** | Blocks you made and blocks other people made of you. |
| **Reports you made** | Your account is removed from them. We keep the reports themselves until we have dealt with them (see "What we keep"). |
| **Other account records** | Your agreement to the chat rules, which chats you've read and muted, your devices registered for notifications, and notifications waiting to be sent to you. |
| **On your iPhone** | The saved groups, invitation records and unfinished drafts that Resbite kept on the iPhone you used. |

### Deleted while we finish (usually minutes, always within 30 days)

| What | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Your sign-in account** | Your verified phone number, your email address, your password, and the link to your Apple or Google account. |
| **Sign in with Apple** | We tell Apple to end Resbite's access to your Apple ID. |
| **Your profile photos** | The image files are deleted from our storage. A photo link that someone opened just before can keep working for a few minutes. |
| **Cover photos of resbites you hosted** | The image files are deleted from our storage, within 7 days. |
| **Your 18+ and Terms record** | Deleted with your sign-in account. |
| **Your profile and co-host roles** | Your profile record is deleted, and with it any co-host role you held or gave. |

## What we keep, and for how long

Some records stay for a limited time after your account is deleted. We keep them only for the reasons given, and then delete them.

| What | Why we keep it | How long |
|---|---|---|
| **Reports other people made about you or your messages**, with a copy of what was reported | To deal with safety concerns, to show what action we took, and to answer regulators | Until we close the report, then 180 days |
| **Reports you made**, no longer linked to your account | So we can finish dealing with them, and show what action we took | Until we close the report, then 180 days |
| **Your deletion receipt**: a request number, your account's internal ID, a scrambled form of the recovery code and the request's dates. Not your messages, contacts or photos | So you can check that deletion is complete, and so we can delete your account again if we ever restore a backup | 30 days after deletion is complete |
| **Backups** | To recover the service after a fault | 7 days |
| **Our hosting providers' technical logs** | To keep the service running and secure | Up to 7 days |
| **Emails and forms you sent us**, and our replies | To deal with your request and any follow-up | 24 months after we close your case |
| **Resbites you hosted that have already happened**, as a blank record with no title, details or host name | So your guests' own history makes sense | 12 months after the resbite ended, or after a group's last week |
| **The "Message deleted" placeholders** in chats | So other people's conversations still make sense | Until the chat's own deletion date: 90 days after a one-off's chat closes, or 12 months after a group message was sent |
| **Records of notifications we sent other people** about something you did | To make sure notifications were delivered | 30 days after sending |
| **Phone numbers of people you invited who don't use Resbite** | So they can still answer, until the invitation ends | Until they answer or the invitation is cancelled, and no later than 30 days after the resbite ends |
| **Phone numbers of people removed from your resbites** | So they aren't invited back by mistake | 30 days after a one-off ends, or 90 days after a group's last week |
| **A record of a suspension**, if your account was suspended: the reason and the dates, no longer linked to your account | So we can show a regulator what we did and why | 12 months after the deletion |

Records that our email provider (Resend) and our text-message provider (Twilio) keep about messages they sent you, such as sign-in codes, are deleted on their own schedules. Our [Privacy Policy](https://link.resbite.com/privacy) lists them.

### Legal holds

Sometimes the law requires us to keep certain records for longer. For example, the police or Ofcom may have asked us to preserve them, or we may need them for a legal claim or a serious safety report. We call this a legal hold.

Even under a legal hold, your account still closes straight away, and everything not covered by the hold is deleted on time. Only the records covered by the hold wait, and only for as long as needed. We'll tell you if a hold delays part of your deletion, unless the law stops us telling you.

### If your account was suspended

You can delete your account while it is suspended. We keep the reports that led to the suspension for their usual period (until we close them, then 180 days), and a record of the suspension's reason and dates, no longer linked to your account, for 12 months after the deletion.

### What we can't delete

- Screenshots or copies other people made, and notifications already delivered to their phones.
- Messages other people sent, and resbites other people host. They belong to those people.
- Entries you added to your iPhone's Calendar. Delete them in the Calendar app.
- Your Apple ID or Google account itself. You can also remove Resbite from the list of apps connected to your Apple ID or Google account in their settings.

## Your messages

When you delete your account:

- the words of every message and announcement you sent are removed straight away, in every chat;
- each one shows as "Message deleted", so the conversation around it still makes sense;
- your name no longer appears; chat lines that mention you say "Someone";
- the empty placeholders are deleted on the chat's normal schedule (see the table above);
- if someone reported one of your messages, the report keeps a copy of it for the report's period.

Messages other people sent stay, because they are theirs.

---

## Your resbites and groups as a host

- **Resbites that haven't happened yet are cancelled.** Every guest gets a notification that the resbite was cancelled.
- **Weekly groups end.** The next week's resbite is cancelled and no new weeks are made. Co-hosts don't take over the group. If you want a group to carry on, ask a co-host to start a new one before you delete your account.
- **Resbites that have already happened stay as a blank record** for your guests: no title, description, cover photo, notes, meeting details, area or host name. We delete them 12 months after they ended.
- **Invitations you sent end.** People you invited by phone number who don't use Resbite have their numbers deleted on the schedule above.

While a host's account is suspended rather than deleted, their weekly groups pause.

## Resbites and groups you joined

You leave every resbite and group you joined straight away, and your guest-list entries go. People you brought along stay on the guest list. Any co-host role you held ends when we finish deleting your account.

---

## On your iPhone

- Deleting your account clears the saved groups, invitation records and unfinished drafts that Resbite kept on the iPhone you used, and signs you out.
- A small deletion receipt stays on that iPhone so you can check that deletion is complete. iPhones can keep an app's secure data even after the app is removed, so the receipt may stay after you delete the app. It holds only identifiers for your request, not your messages, contacts or photos.
- If you used Resbite on another iPhone, your account stops working there too. To clear what Resbite saved on that phone, sign out there or delete the app.

---

## Coming back

Once deletion is complete, you can create a new account at any time. Nothing from your old account comes back.

---

## Other choices about your data

You don't have to delete your account to use your other rights.

- **A copy of your data:** in the app, **Profile → Data & privacy → Request a copy of my data** opens an email to us, ready to send. Or email **privacy@resbite.com**.
- **Correcting your data:** change your name and photo yourself in **Profile**. To change the phone number or email address you sign in with, email **privacy@resbite.com**.
- **Objecting, or limiting how we use your data:** email **privacy@resbite.com**.

We answer within one month. Our [Privacy Policy](https://link.resbite.com/privacy) explains all your rights.
