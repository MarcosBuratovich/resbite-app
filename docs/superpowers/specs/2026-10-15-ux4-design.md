# UX4: profile, settings, sign-in and Wellness adopt the kit

Date: 15 October 2026. Status: **draft for owner review**. The design was agreed in chat on 15 October 2026: three mockup choices and four parts, each answered "looks right", "yes, looks right" or "perfect".

Inputs:
- the approved [UX refresh spec](2026-10-07-ux-refresh-design.md): UX-P1 ("Profile, settings and auth adopt the new components later (UX4)") and its design principles;
- the UX1 component kit (`mobile/src/design/kit.tsx`);
- a map of the 13 screens in scope;
- the owner's choices in the visual-companion mockups, kept in `.superpowers/brainstorm/`, which is not committed.

## Intent

The owner finds the screens wordy ("too many text and button text") and wants them to feel like widgets and components: minimal, calm and beautiful. UX1 delivered that for the event screens. UX4 brings the remaining 13 screens to the same standard, without changing how anything works.

**Success:**
- On the owner's iPhone, each UX4 screen has one heading of three words or fewer, uses kit chips, tiles, rows and cards instead of paragraphs and long button labels, and passes the Larger Text, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion checks.
- Sign-in, sign-up, confirmation, password reset, the account access check, profile name and photo, account details, notifications, privacy, account deletion and Wellness behave exactly as before.
- Wording that protects people keeps its exact text.

## Scope

**Screens (13):**
- Profile tab;
- Account details (setup and edit modes);
- Notifications;
- Data & privacy;
- Delete account;
- Wellness tab;
- landing (`app/index.tsx`);
- introduction;
- sign-in, covering sign in, sign up, reset and confirm (`app/auth/index.tsx`);
- the email-link page (`app/auth/callback.tsx`);
- the account access check (`app/account.tsx`).

The tab bar keeps its native tabs and labels.

**Not in UX4:**
- UX3, which ships with B1: link previews, reminders and calendar;
- real wellness tracking (milestone B5);
- switching account deletion on, which stays behind `EXPO_PUBLIC_ACCOUNT_DELETION_ENABLED`;
- Sign in with Apple;
- Android;
- any database, service or native change.

## Approach

- **App only.** No database change, no new npm dependency and no native configuration change. Nothing touches the live project.
- **Logic moves first, then each screen is redrawn**, as in UX1:
  - `app/auth/index.tsx`'s state, effects and handlers move unchanged into a sign-in helper hook. This covers the four modes, register steps, cooldowns through `confirmationStore`, `checkEmailConfirmation`, Google OAuth and the generation guards.
  - `app/(tabs)/profile.tsx`'s logic moves unchanged into a Profile helper hook. This covers the profile load and save, the versioned photo change and its keep, retry and remove actions, the signed-URL recovery, the pending-invite redirect and sign-out.
  - Every existing browser script must pass unchanged against the moved logic before any screen changes.
- **New kit components** in `mobile/src/design/kit.tsx`, shown in the development-only `/kit` gallery:
  - **Grouped rows:** a card of rows, each with an icon in a tinted circle, a short label and an optional chevron. Rows are at least 44 pt tall and have a destructive variant.
  - **Field card:** the large input card from the creation steps (`CardInput` in `PlanSteps.tsx`), promoted to the kit and shared with sign-in, sign-up, account details and deletion.
  - **Topic sheet:** a row that opens an `InfoSheet` with longer, unchanged text.
  - **Status card:** an icon, a short state title and an optional chip.
- **Kept everywhere:** the existing design principles, tokens and accessibility rules from UX1.
  - One heading per screen.
  - Icon-first, short actions.
  - Status as chips.
  - Explanations behind ⓘ.
  - No text over pictures.
  - No Reanimated layout animations; a test guards this.
  - Curly apostrophes.

### Wording that keeps its exact text

These may move into a sheet, behind ⓘ or into a card, but are not rewritten:
- the Data & privacy statements;
- account deletion warnings, availability text and receipt and status messages (`describe()`);
- account access messages (unconfirmed, access pending, restore);
- confirmation and recovery notices (`confirmationNotice`, `recoveryNotice`), `authLinkGuidance` output and auth validation errors;
- photo save, pending, recovery and cleanup messages (including `profilePhotoStore` copy);
- the RegistrationDetails privacy lines ("not displayed on shared plans", "does not enable SMS sign-in");
- the notification permission copy (`permissionCopy`) and the delivery explanation;
- the sign-out error banner and the leave-without-saving prompts;
- the Wellness disclaimer ("…do not track your activity or measure health").

Headings, button labels, eyebrow labels, taglines and the introduction text may change.

## Screens

### Profile (UX4-D2)
- **Heading:** "Profile".
- **Photo and name card:**
  - The photo (or an initial avatar, no longer marked as a heading) has a camera badge. Tapping it chooses or changes the photo, and a small trash icon removes it.
  - While a photo change needs confirmation, today's recovery panel (Keep saved photo / Retry) replaces this card, with the same wording.
  - The ✎ next to the name opens a sheet with the name field card and Save. Save and error messages keep their wording, and the pending-invite redirect after saving is unchanged.
- **Rows:** People & groups, Account details, Notifications and Data & privacy.
- **Sign out:** a red row in its own card, with the same error handling as today.
- **Preview:** Account details is hidden, "Sample conversation" is a row and "Leave preview" replaces Sign out.
- **Removed:** "Browse ideas", because the Create tab already does it.

### Account details
- **Heading:** "About you" in setup mode, "Account details" in edit mode.
- **Fields:** field cards for birthday, phone and city. Interests become tappable multi-select chips instead of checkboxes, with the same options and the same values saved.
- **Actions:**
  - "Save" is the one primary button. Setup mode adds a "Skip" chip, which still saves empty optional fields.
  - The privacy note moves behind ⓘ.
  - "Clear details" becomes a quiet red row.
  - "Reload saved details" appears only when loading failed.
- **Kept:** the leave-without-saving prompt.

### Account access check (`app/account.tsx`)
- **Layout:** one status card per state: Checking, Confirm your email, Waiting for access (access pending), Let's try again (error), and Restoring your invitation.
- **Headings:** three words or fewer.
- **Guidance:** today's guidance stays word for word, on the card or behind ⓘ.
- **Actions** become chips: Check again, Email help, Details and Sign out.
- **Unchanged:** routing to details, Profile, the invite or Create.

### Wellness (UX4-D5)
- **Heading:** "Wellness". A prominent "Sample data" chip, with ⓘ opening the disclaimer.
- **Content:** a ring card ("2h 30m · together this week") and one card of category bars: Creative 60 min, Physical 60 min, Community 30 min, in each category's tint and ink.
- **No real data.**

### Data & privacy
- **Heading:** "Privacy".
- **Topic rows:** On this device, Account & shared plans, and Preview & samples. Each opens a topic sheet with today's exact text for that section.
- **Deletion:** "Delete account" becomes a red row that opens the deletion screen. The old "Account deletion" section's text, unchanged, follows the deletion screen's own warnings in its "What happens" sheet.

### Notifications
- **Heading:** "Notifications".
- **Content:** one status card ("Not active yet") with a chip for the phone permission state.
- **Actions:** "Check again" and "Settings" chips. Both are hidden in preview, as today.
- **ⓘ** opens the delivery explanation.

### Delete account (UX4-D4)
- **Heading:** "Delete account".
- **Warning:** a short warning card stays visible, holding the first warning sentence as it reads today. "What happens" opens a sheet with the full warnings.
- **Steps:**
  1. "Confirm it's you": a password field card, or "Use an email code instead", with today's flow.
  2. "Type DELETE".
- **Action:** one red "Permanently delete account" button. The receipt, status check, disabled and preview states keep their behaviour and text.

### Landing (UX4-D3)
- **Layout:** the Resbite mark, one picture card with no text over it, the heading "Get together" and one line ("Small plans with your people").
- **Actions:** a "Get started" button and an "I have an account" link.
- **Kept:**
  - "Check account deletion" as a chip when a deletion receipt exists;
  - the development-only "Preview the design" entry, still hidden in release builds.
- **Fixed:** "I have an account" opens sign-in with a valid mode (today it passes `signin`).

### Introduction (UX4-D3)
- **Slides:** three, each a picture card with a two-word title and one line. Today's text is trimmed, using today's illustrations.
- **Controls:** dots, Skip and Next. The last slide's button says "Create account".
- **Fixed:** Back on the first slide returns to the landing safely.

### Sign-in (UX4-D3)
- **Sign in:**
  - "Welcome back", email and password field cards with show-password, and one "Sign in" button.
  - "Forgot password?" and "Create account" links, a "Continue with Google" chip, and "Need to confirm your email?".
  - The private-beta line ("access is opened by invitation") moves behind ⓘ.
- **Sign up** becomes step cards with a progress bar. The fields, checks and order match today's three steps:
  1. "Your name?": name, plus birthday (optional).
  2. "About you": phone, city and interests (tappable chips, as in Account details), all optional, with a Skip chip.
  3. "Your account": email and password plus the Google chip. This step ends with "Create account".

  Validation errors keep their wording. The RegistrationDetails helper lines move behind "Why we ask" ⓘ.
- **Check your inbox / Confirm your email / Reset password:**
  - status cards with short headings;
  - notices and guidance unchanged;
  - actions as chips (I've confirmed, Resend, Use a different email);
  - the resend countdown is shown on the chip.
- **Email-link page:** status cards for Confirming, Choose a new password (a field card and Save) and Let's try again. The guidance keeps its wording.
- **Focus:** switching between sign in, sign up and reset moves VoiceOver focus to the new heading.

## Testing

- **Logic move:** after the two helper hooks are extracted, every existing browser script passes unchanged.
- **Unit tests** for the new pure helpers:
  - sign-up step rules (which fields each step needs, using today's validation);
  - state-to-wording maps (account access check, notifications, the email-link page);
  - the Wellness sample rows.
- **Browser scripts** are updated deliberately as each screen changes:
  - `verify-auth`, `verify-registration`, `verify-confirmation`, `verify-signup-callback`, `verify-account`, `verify-session`;
  - `verify-profile`, `verify-deletion`, `verify-notifications`;
  - the Profile and Wellness steps in `verify-planning`, `verify-people`, `verify-rsvp`, `verify-catalogue` and `verify-covers`;
  - `verify-kit` gains the new kit components.
  - Sign-in tests find fields by label, not by position.
  - Every assertion on protected wording keeps its exact string.
- **Screen captures:** `review-ui` adds every UX4 screen at 320 and 390 pt and with Larger Text, with no sideways scrolling and no clipped text.
- **Full checks:** `npm run check`, all browser scripts, the layout-animation guard, the iOS export and the diff check.

## Rollout and acceptance

1. Build on the `ux4` branch following the implementation plan, with reviews as in UX1 and UX2.
2. Serve the new JavaScript to the owner's development build. The owner then checks on the iPhone:
   - sign out and back in;
   - the landing page and the introduction;
   - starting a sign-up and backing out;
   - requesting a password reset email;
   - editing the Profile name and photo;
   - Account details, Privacy, Notifications, Wellness and Delete account (deletion stays switched off);
   - Larger Text and VoiceOver on a few screens.

   Live-data traces (for example a name or photo change) are confirmed with read-only queries.
3. QA record in `qa/ux4-2026-10-15/`, then merge.
4. UX4 reaches TestFlight in a later build, after the coworker's UX2 invitee check on the current build, so that check isn't disrupted.

**Rollback:** serve the previous `main` JavaScript. There is no database change.

## Risks

- **The sign-in logic is delicate:** cooldowns, generation guards, the confirmation store and OAuth. Moving the logic before any visual change, with the unchanged browser scripts as the gate, contains this.
- **Many tests assert today's wording.** Each assertion changes in the same step as its screen, and protected wording keeps its exact assertions.
- **13 screens in one branch is a large review (UX4-D1).** The plan keeps tasks per screen group, each with its own review.
